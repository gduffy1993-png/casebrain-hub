CASE: R v Liam Carter 
DATE OF ARREST: 03/02/2025 
TIME OF ARREST: 20:15 hours 
LOCATION OF ARREST: Middleton Road, Heywood 
ARRESTING OFFICERS: PC Shaw 22415, PC Turner 19872 
CUSTODY SUITE: Bury Custody Suite 
CUSTODY REF: 25/02/BC/194 
At 19:42 hours on 03/02/2025, officers were dispatched to a reported burglary at 14 
Alderbank Close. A description was circulated of a male wearing a grey puffer jacket seen 
on CCTV approaching the address shortly before the offence. 
At approximately 20:15 hours, while conducting area searches, officers observed a lone 
male walking southbound on Middleton Road. The male was wearing a grey puffer-style 
jacket with the hood down. Officers noted that the male appeared to avoid eye contact and 
placed his hands inside his pockets as the police vehicle approached. 
The male was stopped and identified as Liam Carter, DOB 14/09/1999. He appeared 
nervous and gave short answers. Officers conducted a stop search under Section 1 PACE 
on the grounds that he matched the general build and clothing description of the male seen 
on CCTV. 
The search revealed: 
– No stolen property 
– No tools 
– No gloves 
– No weapons 
– No items linking him to the burglary 
Despite this, officers believed the male’s clothing and build were sufficiently similar to the 
CCTV footage. Due to the proximity to the scene (0.6 miles), the time frame, and the 
ongoing investigation, the male was arrested on suspicion of burglary. 
Carter was cautioned and made no reply. 
He was transported to Bury Custody Suite at 20:32 hours. 
Upon arrival, he was booked in by the custody sergeant. He declined legal advice initially but 
later accepted a duty solicitor. He made no complaints about his treatment and no injuries 
were noted. 
A property search was authorised for the clothing worn at the time of arrest. No forensic 
opportunities were identified. No items of evidential value were seized. 
Carter was placed in Interview Room 2 at 22:10 hours. He was offered water and a break.
