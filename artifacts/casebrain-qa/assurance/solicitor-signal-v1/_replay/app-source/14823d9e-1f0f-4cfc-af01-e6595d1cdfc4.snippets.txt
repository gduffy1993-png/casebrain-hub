3. MG5 CASE SUMMARY - OVERVIEW
Marcus Vale was fatally stabbed near the stairwell area at Eastgate Estate on 14 April 2026. The Crown case is that Leon Hale
was seen arguing with Marcus shortly before the fatal injury. CCTV stills show Leon entering the stairwell area at
approximately 22:41 and leaving at approximately 22:47. A 999 call was recorded at 22:52.
The Crown says the timing, the argument, Leon Hale presence, and witness references to a shiny object provide a route to
prove that Leon unlawfully killed Marcus Vale with the necessary intent. The Crown also relies on partial forensic material and
phone material which places Leon near the estate. The phone location material is not said in the current summary to prove the
precise stabbing moment.
Leon accepts being present and accepts arguing with Marcus. He denies bringing a knife and says Marcus or another male
had a knife. He says he pushed Marcus away, fled during chaos, and did not intend to kill or cause really serious harm. He
