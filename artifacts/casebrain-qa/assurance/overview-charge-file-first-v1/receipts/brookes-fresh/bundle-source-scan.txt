=== SECTION: MG5 CASE SUMMARY ===
On 09 May 2026 Ellie Varna reported receiving WhatsApp messages from a number ending 4471. She stated that the
sender referred to her witness statement in earlier proceedings and told her to 'stop talking'. She provided screenshot
printouts showing a contact named 'T'. One screenshot includes a voice note icon with duration 00:17, but the original
audio file has not been served in this bundle.
Police state that the number ending 4471 is believed to be linked to Taylor Brookes. The basis for that belief is
recorded as previous contact number on custody records and a note from an officer who had dealt with him in March
2026. No subscriber return, handset extraction, SIM registration, device attribution report, or original WhatsApp
export is included in the served papers.
Taylor Brookes was interviewed on 15 May 2026. He denied sending the messages, accepted that he had used a
phone with a SIM ending 4471 at points, and said the phone was shared with Casey Rowe and other people at a flat.
He said he had argued with Ellie Varna previously but denied threatening her or attempting to influence evidence.

=== SECTION: MG11 STATEMENT - ELLIE VARNA ===
I am Ellie Varna. I know Taylor Brookes through people in our estate. I gave a statement to police in April 2026 about
an incident outside Redwood House. On 09 May 2026 I received messages on WhatsApp from a contact saved in my
phone as T. I believed this was Taylor because of how he wrote and because I had spoken to him on that number
before.
The messages said I should 'stop talking' and 'remember who knows where you live'. I was frightened. I took
screenshots and sent them to PC Rowan Vale. I also played a voice note to my sister, but I could not forward it
properly at the time.
I think the first message came in at around 22:45 on Friday 09 May 2026. I later told police in a follow-up call that it
was Saturday night after work. I am not completely sure because I was upset and the messages were on my phone
from the weekend. The screenshot shows 10 May at the top of one screen and 09 May on another screen.
I did not reply apart from sending a question mark. I blocked the contact after taking screenshots. I did not keep a
screen recording. I have not changed my phone since then. The officer took photographs of my screen using his
device and asked me to email the screenshots, which I did.
Statement issues visible on face
Witness says Friday 09 May in one paragraph and Saturday night in another.
Witness belief of sender is based on saved contact name, writing style, and previous use of number.

=== SECTION: INTERVIEW SUMMARY ===
Interview date: 15 May 2026. The defendant was interviewed with a solicitor present. The full audio and transcript are
not included in this bundle; this is a summary note only.
Taylor Brookes denied sending any threatening messages. He said he sometimes used a phone with a number
ending 4471 but that the phone was often left in a shared kitchen at River Yard flats. He named Casey Rowe as
someone who also used the phone to message people. He said he had not seen Ellie Varna for weeks and had no
reason to contact her about court.
When shown the screenshot printout, he said the messages did not prove they came from him. He accepted he had
previously used the contact name 'T' but said anyone could have changed a display name. He denied being the
person speaking on the voice note, but the audio was not played in interview because the original file was not
available.
