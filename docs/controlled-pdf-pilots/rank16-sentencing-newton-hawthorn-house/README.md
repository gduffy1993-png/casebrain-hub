# Rank 16 — Hawthorn House sentencing/Newton design

**Status:** design-only; fictional; source/PDF generation prohibited.

This matter concerns **Marcus Ellery**, a fictional adult caregiver. A designed guilty-plea event is used solely to test sentencing workflow for proposed child-cruelty and assault counts. The parties dispute duration, awareness, the number of incidents and the mechanism of one injury. A Newton hearing may be required, but the hearing route, factual findings, dangerousness assessment and sentence remain blank pending qualified review.

## Design boundary

- PC1–PC2 remain `PROPOSED/NON-OPERATIVE/PENDING QUALIFIED REVIEW`; a workflow plea is not an operative conviction.
- The 190-page plan lies in the approved 120–250 ordinary Crown Court band.
- Victim impact, medical fact, expert opinion, party basis, Newton finding, guideline fact, dangerousness finding and sentence/order are distinct.
- Probation, medical and risk material must remain within discipline and audience limits.
- No filler, simulated judicial conclusion or invented reviewer decision is permitted.
