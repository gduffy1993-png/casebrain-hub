# Rank 18 — Larkfield Magistrates’-to-Crown appeal design

**Status:** design-only; fictional; source/PDF generation prohibited.

This fictional matter concerns **Omar Diriye**, designed as convicted in a Magistrates’ Court of failing to provide a breath specimen. He disputes whether he was physically able to complete the process and whether the machine/operator records support the prosecution account. The appeal package tests notice timing, extension, suspension/stay, record transfer, respondent material and a Crown Court rehearing with focused respiratory evidence.

## Design boundary

- PC1 and every Magistrates’/Crown Court outcome are `PROPOSED/NON-OPERATIVE/PENDING QUALIFIED REVIEW`.
- The 210-page plan lies in the approved 120–300 appeal band.
- The Magistrates’ record is historical; an appeal notice does not itself quash, stay or replace its order.
- Machine output, officer account, medical fact, expert opinion, reasonable-excuse submission and judicial finding remain separate.
- Counsel and expert extracts are mandatory because specialist respiratory evidence is designed as relevant.

They guide fictional structure only.
