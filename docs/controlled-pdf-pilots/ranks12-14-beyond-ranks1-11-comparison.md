# Ranks 12–14: additions beyond ranks 1–11

**Status:** review-only comparison; no generation, remediation or programme-decision authority.

| Rank | New primary coverage | Distinct evidence and formats | Distinct procedure and audience challenge | Non-duplication boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 Copper Lane | Disclosure-led serious case in which open, sensitive, closed, privileged, third-party and inadvertently received material must remain separately governed | MG6-series schedules; controlled intelligence records; native PDF portfolio and embedded-attachment enumeration; digital-signature validation; quarantine/clawback audit; isolated same-name/hash cross-matter negative control | CPIA continuing review, defence requests and third-party material, PII application/open/closed/gist/ruling implementation, late disclosure and privilege lifecycle; an internal-closed boundary that never leaks through solicitor, client, court or CPS outputs without authority | Earlier matters exercise ordinary disclosure, but none makes item-level sensitivity, PII, privilege, embedded children, signature state and cross-matter isolation the central design. Malicious/polyglot files and parser attacks remain solely in the separate security harness. |
| 13 Ember Junction | Fatal road-traffic causation and driving-standard issues, with physical, digital, environmental and medical sources reconciled without automatic inference | Collision survey/reconstruction and scaled plans; vehicle inspection, brake tests and EDR/ECU download; native CSV/XLSX phone, navigation and telematics; signal-controller logs, GIS mapping and pathology | Allocation, bail, expert directions/joint statement, plea and sentencing states; primary admissions/agreed-facts lifecycle, including maker, qualification, signature and court-use separation | Unlike rank 2 homicide, medical evidence is one component of a collision/vehicle/road-system model and does not decide legal causation. Unlike rank 9 digital evidence, device activity must not be equated with driver use. |
| 14 Market House | Compact but complete contested adult summary trial, including an alleged confession and count-level termination outcomes | Custody safeguards, complete interview audio/transcript/gap log; BWV/999/CCTV; damage/value exhibits; witness-contact and summons records; concise filed forms, applications, authorities index, rulings and verdict reasons | Primary PACE confession and section 76/78 lifecycle; witness availability/summons; statutory time limits; hearsay/bad character; discontinuance; no-case submission; dismissal, acquittal and verdict reasons | Malik–Price retains only its frozen interview timing/completeness/custody conflicts and receives no section 78 storyline. Rank 7 remains a youth PACE-safeguard negative control. Rank 14 supplies the distinct adult summary-trial application, order and disposal chain. |

The packages deliberately reuse shared attribution, provenance, service, admissibility, privilege, audience and all-exit contracts. Those are programme-wide safety controls, not duplicate case purposes. Rank 15 retains the dedicated bail/remand/CTL design; ranks 17–18 retain sentencing/appeal specialism; security-only controls do not increase the 18-matter denominator.

They inform fictional structure only; no real-case facts are used.
