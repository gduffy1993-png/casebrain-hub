# Rank 17 — Ivory Garage Court of Appeal design

**Status:** design-only; fictional; source/PDF generation prohibited.

This fictional conviction-appeal matter concerns **Amina Vale**, whose designed Crown Court conviction relates to an allegation that she moved a vehicle and deleted communications to obstruct an investigation. Later witness and vehicle-system evidence may be fresh. The package tests trial-record fidelity, privileged advice, grounds, permission/renewal, extension, fresh-evidence admissibility, conviction safety and count-level outcomes without deciding any of them.

## Design boundary

- PC1–PC2 and every conviction/appeal event are non-operative simulation states pending qualified review.
- The 230-page plan lies in the approved 120–300 appeal band.
- Trial evidence, trial finding, counsel advice, ground, assertion, fresh evidence, admissibility ruling and appellate conclusion remain distinct.
- Privileged advice must never leak to prosecution, court or client-inappropriate outputs; filed grounds do not waive unrelated advice.
- An acquittal, quashing, substitution, retrial or dismissal can be recorded only from an authoritative judgment/order.

Public structural starting points: [Criminal Procedure Rules 2025 Parts 36 and 39](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/criminal-procedure-rules-2025-and-criminal-practice-directions-2023), [Criminal appeal forms](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/criminal-procedure-rules-forms) and [GOV.UK Crown Court appeal overview](https://www.gov.uk/appeal-against-crown-court-verdict/appeal-a-crown-court-decision). They guide fictional structure only.
