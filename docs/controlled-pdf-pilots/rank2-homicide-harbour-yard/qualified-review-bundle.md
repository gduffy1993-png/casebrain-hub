## Fictional matter in neutral terms

Idris Bell died on 16 February 2026 after a service-yard confrontation on 14 February. The prosecution hypothesis is that Daniel Mercer struck him with a metal torch and that Saira Nadeem pushed or kicked him during the sequence. Both defendants dispute the material allegations. CCTV is occluded; witnesses conflict; pathology does not identify an actor or uniquely determine the injury mechanism. Alcohol, prescribed anticoagulant evidence and subsequent treatment are contextual evidence, not AI-determined answers to legal causation.

## 3. Expert boundaries

### Authority and use hierarchy

1. The joint statement is authoritative for the expert agreements/disagreements it records at its date.
2. The latest individual report/addendum governs that expert's individual position where the point is not superseded or covered by the joint statement.
3. Earlier reports remain visible for changes of opinion.
4. Service does not prove admissibility.
5. Judicial rulings govern permitted use, not scientific truth.

### Discipline limits

| Discipline | May address | Must not determine |
|---|---|---|
| Forensic pathology | Injuries, medical cause of death, plausible mechanisms, compatibility and limitations | Actor identity, intent, exact sequence, guilt or final legal causation |
| Neuropathology | Intracranial injury, deterioration sequence and bounded timing/mechanism opinions | External actor/object, exact impact time, intent or legal causation |
| Toxicology | Samples, methods, results, uncertainty and cautious pharmacological interpretation | Exact behaviour, aggression, balance, intent or whether alcohol/medication breaks causation |
| Forensic biology/trace | Examination results, mixture quality, transfer/persistence propositions and continuity limits | Weapon use, deposition time, fatal mechanism, intent or unsupported categorical source attribution |
| Clinical timeline | Recorded observations, treatment events and source timestamps | Criminal negligence, intervening legal cause, homicide route or actor identity |
