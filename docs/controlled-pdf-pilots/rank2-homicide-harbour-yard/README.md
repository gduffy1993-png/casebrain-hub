## Diagnostic purpose

Test a coherent heavy homicide record involving:

- two defendants with different alleged acts and mental states;
- proposed murder and manslaughter routes that must remain non-operative until qualified review;
- uncertain blunt-impact mechanism and causal contribution;
- death after a clinically significant delay;
- pathology, neuropathology and toxicology limitations;
- prosecution and defence expert disagreement plus a joint statement;
- admissions and agreed facts that change state over time;
- strict defendant, source, audience and exit separation.
