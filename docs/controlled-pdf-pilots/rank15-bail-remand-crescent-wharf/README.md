# Rank 15 — Crescent Wharf bail/remand design

**Status:** design-only; fictional; source/PDF generation prohibited.

This dedicated application matter concerns **Leila Okoro**, an adult accused after a fictional commercial-premises burglary at Crescent Wharf. The offence allegations are only the context for first remand, opposed bail, a later variation application and a disputed breach arrest. Risk assertions, defence answers, address and surety evidence, proposed conditions, operative conditions and breach findings must never collapse into one another.

## Design boundary

- PC1–PC2 are `PROPOSED/NON-OPERATIVE/PENDING QUALIFIED REVIEW`.
- The 76-page plan sits in the approved 40–90 application band; authentic need, not quota, controls any future total.
- Exact Bail Act, custody-time-limit, appeal, service and deadline propositions remain for qualified review under law current on the review date.
- No application, allegation or officer account is a judicial finding. A refused, granted, varied or superseded order remains source- and date-specific.
- Security-harness inputs, holdouts and application remediation are outside this package.

## Artefacts

The package contains the fictional skeleton, evidence ownership matrix, eight-document register and matching realism specifications, truth and trap controls, all-audience/all-exit acceptance contracts, blank reviewer decisions and a closed stop gate.

Public structural starting points: [CPS Bail guidance](https://www.cps.gov.uk/prosecution-guidance/bail), [CPS Custody Time Limits guidance](https://www.cps.gov.uk/prosecution-guidance/custody-time-limits), [Criminal Procedure Rules 2025](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/criminal-procedure-rules-2025-and-criminal-practice-directions-2023) and [Criminal Procedure Rules forms](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/criminal-procedure-rules-forms). They guide fictional structure only.
