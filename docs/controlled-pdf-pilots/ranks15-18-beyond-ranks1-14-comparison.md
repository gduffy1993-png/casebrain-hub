# Ranks 15–18: additions beyond ranks 1–14

**Status:** review-only comparison; no generation, remediation or programme-decision authority.

| Rank | New primary coverage | Distinct evidence and formats | Distinct procedure and audience challenge | Non-duplication boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 Crescent Wharf | Bail/remand proof as the matter’s central purpose | Certified antecedent and attendance records; address, employment, treatment, surety/security and means evidence; time-specific court orders; alleged-contact digital evidence; CTL worksheets | First remand, opposed bail, condition design, variation, alleged breach, renewed decision and CTL/appeal states; medical, financial and witness-safety controls | Earlier matters mention bail incidentally. | 16 Hawthorn House | Full sentencing workflow with a materially disputed factual basis | Written plea basis and response; protected child and defence Newton evidence; hearing transcript/ruling; PSR and risk sources; victim statement, paediatric evidence, antecedents, references and means | Plea/basis acceptance, Newton directions/hearing/findings, guideline facts, dangerousness, credit/totality, ancillary orders, sentence record and appeal advice | Unlike rank 2, causation is not tried at a homicide trial. | 17 Ivory Garage | Court of Appeal conviction appeal and historical trial-record fidelity | Certified trial transcript, summing-up, rulings, jury material, verdicts; privileged counsel advice; filed grounds; proposed fresh witness and vehicle-data expert evidence; signature and filing records | Permission, renewal, extension, fresh-evidence application/ruling, conviction safety and count-level quashing/acquittal/substitution/retrial consequences | This is review of a Crown Court conviction on an appellate record. It does not become a retrial or reuse rank 9’s primary digital-attribution purpose; vehicle data is a proposed fresh-evidence issue. |
| 18 Larkfield Appeal | Magistrates’ Court conviction appeal by the distinct Crown Court rehearing route | Magistrates’ record/reasons/orders; breath-device printouts and logs; custody audio/transcript; calibration/maintenance records; respiratory records/report; Crown Court rehearing transcript | Notice scope and deadline, extension, stay/suspension, record transfer, fresh evidential rehearing and consequential conviction/sentence/disqualification/costs orders | Unlike rank 17, the Crown Court receives a Magistrates’ appeal and rehearing record rather than deciding conviction safety on the Crown Court trial record. Unlike rank 13, the road context is incidental; specimen procedure and appellate route are primary. |

Shared attribution, provenance, service, admissibility, privilege, audience and all-exit contracts are deliberate programme-wide controls, not duplicate matter purposes. Malicious/polyglot inputs remain in the separate security harness. The four sealed holdouts remain outside the 18-core denominator and have not been created or exposed.
